# Product Positioning

## What this is

A real-world communication survival tool for Chinese travelers in Japan who
do not speak Japanese.

The product exists to answer, in this order, for a user standing in a real
situation right now:

1. **What should I say?** — the right phrase for this exact situation.
2. **How should I say it naturally in Japan?** — natural, appropriately
   polite Japanese, not a literal or textbook translation.
3. **How can I play/show it immediately?** — audio playback or large
   readable text the user can hand to another person.
4. **How can I keep moving without needing to study Japanese?** — zero
   learning curve, zero memorization, zero grammar.

## What this is not

- Not a language-learning app. No lessons, streaks, quizzes, or grammar
  explanations, ever.
- Not a dictionary. Word-level lookup is not the product; situational
  phrases are.
- Not a generic AI chat app. The interface is not a chatbot; it is a set of
  fast, situational actions that happen to be powered by translation
  technology where needed.
- Not a celebrity fan app. No public-facing content is built around a real
  person's identity, likeness, or brand (see `LEGAL_BRAND_SAFETY.md`).

## Priority order (absolute, from the original brief)

1. Communication reliability
2. Speed
3. Emergency access
4. Translation quality
5. Readability
6. Low cognitive load
7. Offline resilience
8. Accessibility
9. Emotional motivation
10. Visual effects

If aesthetics conflict with usability, the aesthetic loses. If an animation
slows communication, it is removed. Utility is the product; everything else
is in service of it.

## Target user

A Chinese traveler in Japan with little or no Japanese, often under time
pressure, possibly tired, stressed, lost, or traveling alone, who wants to
solve an immediate real-world problem (station, hotel, restaurant, shopping,
help, event, emergency) without studying anything first.

## Emotional layer (brand-safe)

The product retains an original emotional register inspired by abstract
themes — freedom, courage, movement, cosmic atmosphere, stage energy, rock
energy, starlight, independence — expressed entirely through original visual
design, original copy, and (if ever added) licensed or original audio. No
celebrity mascot, likeness, or branding is used to express this register.
See `LEGAL_BRAND_SAFETY.md`.
