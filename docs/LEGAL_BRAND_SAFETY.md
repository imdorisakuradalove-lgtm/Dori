# Legal & Brand Safety

## Decision

For all public-facing branding, this product uses a fully original brand
identity. "DoriGo" is an internal working codename only and must never
appear in App Store/Play Store metadata, in-app copy, marketing assets, or
any other user-facing surface unless a future, separate legal review
explicitly clears it.

## Prohibited, without exception, unless separately and explicitly licensed

- Dori Sakurada's name, likeness, photos, voice, or music.
- Any trademark, logo, or official branding associated with them or their
  representatives.
- Any wording, imagery, or design that implies endorsement, affiliation, or
  sponsorship by a real person or their management/label/agency.
- Any celebrity-like mascot — i.e., an original character whose design,
  styling, or presentation is close enough to a real person to imply that
  person.
- Any copyrighted music, artwork, or footage the team does not hold rights
  to.

## What is retained

The product keeps an original emotional register — freedom, courage,
movement, cosmic atmosphere, stage energy, rock energy, starlight,
independence — expressed only through:

- Original visual design (the dark cosmic design system in
  `ARCHITECTURE.md` / `design-system/`).
- Original UI copy and (if ever added later) original motivational text.
- Original or properly licensed audio, if audio is added in a later phase.

No part of this emotional register may be implemented by referencing,
resembling, or evoking a specific real person's identifiable persona.

## Event scene

The MVP's generic Event scene (see `MVP_SCOPE.md`) supports concerts, live
events, fan meetings, exhibitions, stage events, and conventions in
general. It contains no celebrity- or fandom-specific content, imagery, or
naming. It must remain usable and sensible for any live event, not just one
particular artist's.

## Distribution

Before any public release, confirm store-listing name, icon, screenshots,
and description contain no reference to a real person's name or likeness,
independent of whatever internal codename the project uses. This is a
release-gate checklist item, not a one-time decision made now.

## Open items requiring a human legal decision (not an engineering call)

- Whether to ever pursue a licensing negotiation for Dori Sakurada-specific
  assets for a future, clearly-gated V2 skin.
- Distribution-market-specific legal requirements (e.g., a Chinese app
  store presence) — flagged in `AUDIT.md` §11, unresolved, out of scope for
  engineering to decide unilaterally.
